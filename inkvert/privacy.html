Inkvert – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Developer: Eureka Nexus

Inkvert turns photos of handwritten or printed notes into editable text. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

No account needed

Inkvert does not ask you to sign in or create an account. We do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

Photos and text recognition

When you tap “Convert to text”, the photo you selected is sent over an encrypted connection to Google’s Gemini AI (through Google Firebase) only to read the text in it. The recognized text is sent back to your device. We, the developer, do not receive, view or store your photos or your text on any server. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Translation (Pro)

When you use Translate, the text of that scan is sent the same way to Google’s Gemini AI only to create the translation. We do not receive or store it.

Data stored on your device

Your scans, photos, text, folders and settings are saved only on your device. Deleted scans stay in the in-app Trash for 30 days and are then removed. If you create a backup, the backup file is saved where you choose (for example your Downloads folder) and stays under your control.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a page. Photos are not uploaded anywhere except for the text recognition described above.

Purchases

Inkvert Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google. We do not receive your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether Pro was bought on your Google account, so it can unlock Pro features.

Advertising

Inkvert is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob. Pro users do not see ads. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address and app interaction data to show and measure ads. In regions where consent is required, the app asks for your consent with Google’s consent form, and you can change your choice. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

Inkvert is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

All your content is stored on your device and can be deleted inside the app (Settings → Delete all data, or Settings → Trash) or by uninstalling Inkvert. We do not keep a copy.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
